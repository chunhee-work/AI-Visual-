For the OG10 teaser short, EVERY clip must follow this continuity grammar:

1. **Continuous first-person POV, curiously observing.** The whole film stays in first-person POV, actively and curiously looking around at everything happening in the street. The AUDIENCE *is* that POV — it must feel immersive, "you are there," like the viewer's own eyes. The POV person may WALK / move around when it serves the moment (not always static at the table).

2. **Every clip chains to the next.** Each clip must END with something happening + a camera move/turn, and the NEXT clip OPENS matching/continuing that exact transition — a match cut, so the 30s reads as ONE unbroken POV journey, never disconnected shots.
   - **CRITICAL — the transition trigger is a WORLD EVENT the POV notices and reacts to, NOT an OG10 character.** The OG10 NEVER react to the camera, NEVER turn toward it, NEVER drive the cut. Instead something happens in the world (a sound from a shop, an aroma, an object/person passing, a bell, footsteps) → the first-person POV notices it and turns/moves → transition.
   - **Every trigger must obey REAL-WORLD LOGIC (真实世界逻辑)** — believable, grounded, physically plausible. No floating/levitating objects, no magical cues to drive a transition. Use real sensory events: sounds, smells, people/vehicles passing, light, doors/bells.

3. **Be creative with the transitions** — vary how the camera turns/links each beat (sound cue, motion follow, light bloom, object passing frame, etc.).

4. **First-person's own voice reactions are allowed** (added 2026-06-23). Layer the POV man's OWN diegetic vocal reactions into the native audio to match his actions/beats — a groggy mumble on waking, a soft surprised inhale/"huh?", a curious hum when he turns to look, etc. Keep them English / non-verbal vocalizations, short and natural (not scripted dialogue). They reinforce the immersive "you are the POV" feel.

**Why:** Makes the teaser feel like one continuous immersive first-person experience, pulling the viewer in, rather than a slideshow of separate shots.

**How to apply:** Design each Seedance clip's final beat as a deliberate transition hook, and start the following clip from where that hook left off. Combine with [[og10-teaser-no-face-hands-legs-backview]] (still never show OG10 faces) and [[seedance-style-surface-treatment-block]].
