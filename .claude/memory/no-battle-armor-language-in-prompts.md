**⭐⭐ Rule (locked 2026-06-19):** Moving forward, NEVER write "STILL IN BATTLE ARMOR" / "in their battle armor" / "armored mode" / "in their armor" or similar armor-language prose in Higgsfield image prompts or Seedance video prompts. The canonical character ref (turnaround or lineup) already locks the visual identity including how they look (with or without armor). Repeating armor language in prose makes the model over-emphasize combat readiness or render generic battle armor variations that drift from the chill walking mode + canonical fidelity we want.

**Why:** 2026-06-19 — User directed: "moving forward pls do not mention all STILL IN BATTLE ARMOR, in the prompt". The redundant prose was making renders skew toward fierce/combat aesthetic instead of the relaxed chill walking mode register matching the narrative beat (Sheet 07B = peaceful village arrival, NOT combat).

**How to apply:**

1. **TRUST the canonical ref** — @图片N = character canonical turnaround already shows them in their full visual design (armor, weapons, accessories). Don't restate what the ref already shows.

2. **DESCRIBE THE ACTION not the wardrobe** — write what the character is DOING (walking, sitting, listening, gesturing) not what they ARE WEARING.

3. **ONLY mention armor/wardrobe when there's a STATE CHANGE:**
   - ✅ OK to mention if armor TRANSFORMS into Earth clothes (S20 transformation moment per [[og10-armor-transformation-logic]])
   - ✅ OK to mention if armor is DAMAGED/dirty after combat (state shift from canonical)
   - ✅ OK to mention if hero is shown WITHOUT armor (state shift from canonical)
   - ❌ NOT OK to remind "still in armor" when canonical already shows armor

4. **Remove these phrases from all prompts going forward:**
   - "all STILL IN BATTLE ARMOR"
   - "STILL IN BATTLE ARMOR"
   - "in their battle armor"
   - "in their armor"
   - "in battle armor"
   - "armored mode"
   - "chibi armored mode designs"
   - "all in armor" (when referring to OG10 default appearance)

5. **Replace with action/posture descriptions:**
   - ❌ "HEARTA in purple knight armor walks slowly behind keeper"
   - ✅ "HEARTA walks slowly behind keeper per @图片N canonical"

   - ❌ "OG10 in their battle armor stand out as anachronistic visitors"
   - ✅ "OG10 walk through the peaceful Earth village in respectful cluster formation per @图片N canonical lineup"
