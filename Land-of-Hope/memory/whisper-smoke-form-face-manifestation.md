**⭐ Canonical evolution (locked 2026-06-12):** The Whisper smoke-vapor form (the boss-tier antagonist's true vapor body, distinct from her peasant-disguise human form) has TWO RENDERING MODES depending on the beat:

### Mode 1 — Abstract no-face (calmer / wide-establishing / arrival beats)
- Head-curl is pure abstract smoke silhouette
- NO eyes, NO maw, NO fangs, NO facial features whatsoever
- Lava-cores burning through the head-curl as veins but not forming face
- Use this mode for: wide establishing shots, calmer arrival beats, scenes where Whisper is observed from distance, scenes where face would distract from S-curve silhouette
- This is the previously-locked V3 turnaround style

### Mode 2 — Manifested face (peak boss-reveal / roaring / summoning / commanding)
- Head silhouette MANIFESTS a developed face during peak intensity
- TWO BRIGHT CRIMSON GLOWING EYES (primary pair) — burning intense red, like furnace coals, sometimes elongated and demonic
- ELONGATED SKULL-LIKE MAW curving downward from the upper head — can OPEN WIDE in vertical roar or stay closed in sinister silhouette
- Inside the open maw, glowing crimson lava-core (throat-like inner-glow as if the Whisper is full of magma inside)
- Sharp jagged silhouette edges at the maw suggesting teeth/fangs but stylized as smoke-shapes, not literal teeth
- SECONDARY LAVA-CORE "EYE-SPOTS" scattered through the head-curl and upper body (multiple smaller glowing crimson eye-like cores embedded in the smoke vapor — reads as additional menacing eyes or just intense lava concentrations)
- Smoke-tendrils trail off the head like flowing dark hair
- Use this mode for: peak rage moments, boss-reveal beats, roaring/howling, summoning corrupted villagers, commanding the horde, attack-launch beats

### Shared rules across both modes
- Body is STILL translucent dark indigo-purple flowing smoke-vapor (NOT flesh — even the face is made of smoke + lava-veins, not physical material)
- Body S-curve trunk, two flowing smoke-tendril arms terminating in clawed five-finger hand-shapes
- Crimson molten lava-cores burning through smoke veins like cracks of magma
- Same canonical sizing (towering, looms over villagers)

### Reference renders
- Mode 1 reference: the clean EMPTY-village MCU boss-reveal render with abstract head-curl (2026-06-12 V2)
- Mode 2 reference: the developed-face render with crimson skull-maw + corrupted villager horde foreground (2026-06-12)

### How to apply going forward
- Pick the mode based on the beat's emotional intensity
- Default to Mode 1 for wide/establishing/calmer beats
- Switch to Mode 2 for peak intensity / boss-reveal / roaring / summoning / attack-launch
- When mixing both in a sequence, ARC the manifestation: starts as Mode 1 abstract → escalates to Mode 2 manifested as the Whisper's rage peaks
- For attack-launch sequences, Mode 2 manifested face is appropriate (the rage IS the face)
