**⭐⭐ Rule (locked 2026-06-18):** ALL Seedance video prompts moving forward must have CINEMATIC PACING not RUSHED. Adequate pauses between dialogue lines and emotional beats. Smooth natural movements. Rich expression changes. Don't cram many narrative beats into short clip durations — split into multiple focused clips with breathing room between dialogue/action.

**Why:** 2026-06-18 — User explicitly said: "for this scene 10 second is too fast and Old man should talk slow. 要有电影的节奏，而不是赶。要有适量的停顿。Smooth and natural movements, Rich expression changes." After delivering 10s Hospital Wake clip that crammed 6 narrative panels with dialogue too tightly.

**How to apply:**

1. **Pacing principle:** Cinema-quality clips give scenes BREATHING ROOM. Each dialogue line should have its emotional weight + visual pause. NOT machine-gun delivery.

2. **Split long narratives into multiple clips:**
   - Sheet with 6 panels of dialogue/action → 4-6 separate Seedance clips (not 1 long clip)
   - Each clip focuses on 1-2 narrative beats with proper pacing
   - Per [[seedance-cinematic-continuity-rules]] — 3-5s focused clips work best

3. **Dialogue pacing within clips:**
   - Each spoken line gets 0.5-1s pause after for emotional impact
   - Old/wise characters speak SLOW measured (longer pauses between phrases)
   - Young/cocky characters can speak more naturally paced
   - Group cascade dialogue: stagger lines with pauses between speakers

4. **Visual pacing rhythm:**
   - Establishing shots: hold longer to let viewer absorb scene (2-3s)
   - Close-up emotional beats: hold on expression change (1.5-2s)
   - Transition beats: smooth camera glide with adequate duration

5. **Movement pacing:**
   - "Smooth natural movements" = gradual + organic motion, not abrupt
   - Characters wake up slowly + groggy, not jolt awake
   - Walking pace measured, not rushed
   - Hand gestures gentle + careful

6. **Expression change pacing:**
   - "Rich expression changes" = let each emotion register fully
   - Don't snap from one expression to another instantly
   - Build emotional shifts gradually (confusion → realization → concern)

7. **Apply to BOTH:**
   - Seedance video prompts (primary)
   - Higgsfield image storyboard sheets (composition pacing/breathing room)

8. **Standard Seedance clip duration guide:**
   - Single beat focused: 4-5s (Seedance minimum 4s)
   - 2-3 dialogue exchanges: 6-8s
   - Multi-character cascade: 6-8s with pauses
   - Old wise character dialogue: 6-10s slower pace
   - NEVER cram 6 panels of narrative into 10s — split into 4-6 clips

**Examples of what NOT to do:**
- ❌ 10s clip covering establishing + first wake + look around + 3 voices + boy realization + old man approach + dialogue (too rushed)
- ❌ Multiple dialogue lines back-to-back with no pause
- ❌ Old wise character speaking quickly without measured pace

**Examples of correct application:**
- ✅ Split 6-panel sheet into 4-6 separate 4-7s clips
- ✅ Each clip has clear breathing room + pause register
- ✅ Old man speaks slow measured with "Mmm..." filler pauses
- ✅ Dialogue lines spaced with 0.5-1s emotional pause between
