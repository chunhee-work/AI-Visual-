**LOCKED 2026-06-03** — user explicitly directed: **"we take this lighting as reference"** after multiple iterations where cool blue dawn lighting was too dark, bright afternoon felt too flat, and warm sunset over the plaza was too dramatic. The approved version is the soft warm golden hour / soft sunset hybrid in the WICK indoor/window setting.

## The locked WICK-window lighting palette

### Sky and atmosphere
- **Sky**: soft painterly pink-peach-coral-lavender twilight gradient — warm honey near the horizon transitioning up to soft lavender-blue with delicate streaks of pink-peach-coral cloud layers
- **Soft white-pale cumulus clouds** drifting across with warm-lit edges
- **Soft pale moons** (one or two) hanging in the upper warmer twilight sky — subtle, dreamlike
- **Distant fantasy mountains** catch soft pink-peach alpenglow on snow-capped peaks, gentle aerial perspective
- **Distant fantasy castles / floating spires / floating islands** subtly visible in the warm haze

### Key light direction
- One unified motivated key light — **soft warm low golden hour sun** coming through the window from outside, slightly angled (late afternoon → soft sunset transition)
- Light streams INTO the boy's interior through the window
- Soft warm honey-amber light bathes the interior stone walls
- Subtle warm rim light on the boy's hair tufts, ear edge, back/shoulder of his patched tunic
- No harsh directional shadows — soft falloff throughout

### Color palette
- **Interior**: warm honey-amber stone walls, warm sandy-cream window-arch carving glows softly
- **Boy**: hair tufts catch warm rim light, soft warm bounce on his back/shoulder, no harsh shadow on his back
- **Exterior plaza** (through the window): plaza statues catch warm honey-amber on sun-side, soft lavender-cool shadows on shaded side, the cyan crystal flames on pillars glow softly against the warm twilight, brass lanterns glow warm
- **Restrained saturation** — clean painterly twilight tones, NEVER neon or oversaturated

### Decorative atmospheric elements
- A few stray **pink flower petals** drifting gently through the warm interior air (signature WICK indoor mood element)
- Small clay pot of **lavender flowers** on the windowsill (the lavender holds its color in the warm light)
- Sprig of **green ivy** curling along the inside of the sill
- Small **clay pottery** on a carved interior shelf (soft blur in background)

### Mood
- **Quiet hopeful reverence** — warm, intimate, dreamlike
- The warm interior cocoon framing the boy in safety while he gazes out at the distant legendary plaza bathed in the same warm sunset
- Unified warm palette across the whole frame (not the warm-cool contrast version we tried earlier — that was rejected)

## How to apply

When building any WICK indoor / window scene prompt going forward:
1. Paste the locked **SHARED STYLE** + **SOFT CINEMATIC LIGHTING** blocks from [[land-of-hope-locked-visual-style]] as base
2. THEN add this scene-specific lighting override:

```
⚠️ WARM GOLDEN HOUR / SOFT SUNSET TWILIGHT — LOCKED WICK INDOOR LIGHTING:
The scene is bathed in the LOCKED WICK indoor warm twilight lighting — soft painterly pink-peach-coral-lavender twilight sky with warm honey near the horizon transitioning to soft lavender-blue upper sky, delicate streaks of pink-peach-coral cloud layers, soft white-pale cumulus clouds with warm-lit edges, soft pale moons hanging in the upper warm twilight sky. Distant mountains catch soft pink-peach alpenglow. One unified motivated key light — soft warm low golden hour / soft sunset sun coming THROUGH the window from outside, late afternoon → soft sunset transition. Light streams into the interior — warm honey-amber bathing the interior stone walls, warm sandy-cream window-arch carving glowing softly, subtle warm rim light on the boy's hair tufts / ear edge / back-shoulder of his patched tunic. Plaza statues outside catch warm honey-amber on sun-side, soft lavender-cool shadows on shaded side. Restrained saturation — clean painterly twilight tones, never neon. Unified warm palette across the whole frame. Quiet hopeful reverence, warm intimate dreamlike mood.

DECORATIVE WICK INDOOR ELEMENTS: a few stray pink flower petals drifting gently through the warm interior air, small clay pot of lavender flowers on the windowsill, sprig of green ivy curling along the inside of the sill, small clay pottery hint on a carved interior shelf (soft blur in background).
```

3. Then add the rest of the scene description.

- **Bright clear afternoon lighting** — too flat, lacks emotional warmth
- **Pure warm orange sunset over the plaza** — also too dramatic, overrode the locked plaza palette
- **Warm interior + cool exterior contrast** — visually busy, splits the eye, lacks unity
