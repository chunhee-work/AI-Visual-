**⭐ Rule (locked 2026-06-15):** For all OG10 group scenes where the heroes are confronting the Whisper threat, ALL OG10 characters must have their BACK TO THE CAMERA. They face TOWARD the Whisper / horde in the distance ahead of them, NOT toward the camera.

**Why:** 2026-06-15 — User explicitly said "OG10 should not look at the camera, they should look at whisper, OG10 back to the Camera (keep this in memory when generating storyboard image)." This is the classic action-movie composition for hero-vs-threat moments — the audience camera is positioned behind the heroes so we see what they see + share their POV looking at the threat. Looking at camera breaks the dramatic tension.

**How to apply:**

1. **For all OG10 group formation shots facing Whisper:**
   - Camera positioned BEHIND or BESIDE the heroes
   - We see the BACKS of all heroes (back of heads, back of shoulders, back of armor/clothing)
   - Heroes look forward TOWARD the distant Whisper / horde threat
   - The Whisper is visible in the distance ahead (the direction the heroes face)

2. **For mixed shots with some heroes leading non-combat villagers:**
   - The protecting heroes still face the Whisper (back to camera)
   - The guiding heroes (e.g., ECOX + HEARTA leading villagers to safety) can be in 3/4 or side-profile as they direct villagers but should NOT face camera
   - Villagers being protected can face any direction (they're not the heroes)

3. **Exceptions where heroes CAN face the camera:**
   - Pure portrait/character close-ups (Sheet 04B face close-ups style)
   - Single-character hero moments before combat
   - Cinematic dialogue beats between heroes
   - Aftermath shots after threat is gone

4. **For OG10 trailer Sheet 05 + beyond:**
   - Convergence + formation scenes = back to camera
   - Defensive line scenes = back to camera
   - Final stand scenes = back to camera
   - Charging-toward-Whisper scenes = back to camera

5. **Composition tips:**
   - Hero silhouettes from behind create powerful unified front
   - Whisper visible looming in distance creates threat scale
   - Distant horde silhouettes between heroes + Whisper add depth
   - Cobblestone + lava-cracks in middle ground
