New + revised visual designs locked for the [[land-of-hope-trailer]]. All in the locked trailer standard: **stylized-but-realistic Pixar/Disney animated-feature finish + real flawed skin on humans** (see [[character-turnaround-style]], [[cinematic-keyart-standard]]). Turnarounds use the locked **6-panel 3×2 layout** on a clean soft studio backdrop. Deliver every prompt bilingually per [[bilingual-mandarin-responses]].

**HEARTA — NEWEST locked look (2026-05-26, SUPERSEDES the old "pink witch" descriptor):** a cute, chibi-leaning **stylized-but-realistic Pixar/Disney** support girl with **real flawed skin** (fine pores, peach fuzz, light freckles, natural asymmetry — not plastic/not a glossy doll). Long flowing wavy **lavender-to-pink** hair; big sparkly **violet** eyes; gentle sweet smile. **Glowing purple halo** above the head. **Purple-and-gold heart-magic armor** with a glowing pink **heart emblem** on the chest; sparkly layered purple skirt; purple fingerless gloves; purple armored boots with pink heart accents. Signature prop = a **golden winged staff topped with a glowing pink crystal heart**. Built via a 3-image merge (garment+color ref / face+hair-color ref / chibi render-standard ref), then re-rendered for flawed skin + Pixar finish. This is her canonical look now; her turnaround uses a clean soft light-lavender studio backdrop.

**WICK (NEW — the 11th boy, the story's emotional anchor; later launches the ark):** small timid stylized-but-realistic Pixar/Disney boy; messy soft brown hair; huge worshipful eyes; humble slightly-oversized **patched coarse-cloth tunic** + simple trousers + worn little boots; cups a **little sputtering mechanical spark-gadget** — revealed (S10, ⭐signature) to be the core of **the old ark**: a battered-but-beautiful **LITTLE** ship WICK secretly rebuilt from salvage, hidden in a leaning barn, with a blue engine glow. He flies it into the battle to evacuate the 10; the heroes board reluctantly (one covers the retreat and boards last). Then WICK drops the launch remote, leaps back down to grab it, is caught by a spellbound villager and can't pull free — so, trapped, he presses the button himself to launch the ark and send the 10 to safety: **his sacrifice**. (The ark is a small rebuilt ship — NOT a huge hangar vessel.) Single hero base + 6-panel turnaround built (warm-neutral studio backdrop).

**Villager (NEW — the Whisper's first victim, shots S4–S5):** ordinary kindly stylized-but-realistic Pixar/Disney village woman; dark hair in a **low bun** w/ loose strands; humble **cream patched peasant dress** + **maroon fringed shawl** + worn brown lace-up shoes; gentle warm face. **Corrupted variant** (S5): snapped-open **glowing red eyes** + unnatural red catch-light + a subtly-wrong menacing smile + oily smoke wisps at the ear + **fingernails with a subtle, sickly gradient red at the tips — NOT bright or glowing, but a dull darkened rotting/necrotic discoloration creeping up the nail** (corruption invading the fingers) — built two-image with The Whisper. This is the **reusable corruption signature** for ANY infected character as the red "leaps to the next person, and the next" (S5 link). Base + turnaround built.

**The Whisper (NEW — the corruption motif; "The Whisper" is a placeholder name pending):** a serpentine tendril of **oily blue-black smoke** with a sick **red glow** pulsing deep inside, moving with eerie snake-like purpose; the visual carrier of the corruption. Spreads two ways: (1) it pours into a sleeping victim's ear (S4, the quiet first infection); (2) once turned, the infected can dissolve into whisper-smoke, travel, reform, then VIOLENTLY seize and tear at the next victim — black-red smoke forced in through the brutal grip like ink blooming in water; the victim convulses, back arching, eyes bursting red, then instantly turns to attack the next (S6 reworked = **撕裂 / "The Tearing"**: "the whisper no longer asks — it takes"). The earlier gentle-handshake S6 was REPLACED by this violent version. First render came out photoreal → restyled to our stylized-but-realistic painterly animated-feature finish.

**Fountain of Life (NEW key asset, 2026-05-28 — REPLACES the Mayor figure):** the town's central monument + value/productivity engine. A circular stone fountain; at its heart a floating multi-faceted **Crystal of Value (TON)** whose glow is the source of the fountain's elegant white light-water jet; **engraved plate-medallions around the basin rim = productivity multipliers** — more plates → stronger white light radiating over the Earth town (brightens as the town prospers). Art-ref: ornate stone basin, ring of framed plates, white sparkling jet, glowing lamps, night town with CCATS shopfronts + blue crown-crest banners. EVERY Earth-town shot centers it.

**Old knowledge man (NEW character, 2026-05-28 — REPLACES the Mayor-as-guide):** a kindly, wise old elder/keeper who leads OG10 through the town's lessons (the Fountain/TON + the Wall of Names). Proposed look (pending confirm): aged with warm real flawed/wrinkled skin, simple scholarly robes, long soft white beard, kind eyes, maybe round spectacles + a worn book or small lantern; humble but dignified; our stylized-but-realistic Pixar/Disney style.
