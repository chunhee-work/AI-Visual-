**Rule (locked 2026-06-09):** Higgsfield's safety filter is strict. Even though Land of Hope is painterly Pixar-register dark fantasy with NEVER-KILL constraint, certain word/scenario combinations trip the NSFW flag and the prompt won't generate. Soften language proactively when describing menace, corruption, or threat — keep the narrative impact but rewrite the words.

**Why:** 2026-06-09 — Sheet 1 OG10 convergence prompt failed Higgsfield safety check. User: "the prompt u give me cannot work on higgsfiled it showing not safe for work, pls make sure it work." ## Softening playbook for Land of Hope dark fantasy scenes

1. **Reframe corruption as FANTASY MAGIC, not HORROR**:
   - The corrupted figures are "magical adversaries with red-amber glowing eyes and crimson chi energy aura" — NOT "horror-corrupted villagers with blazing red eyes and dark blood-like mist"
   - This is the painterly Pixar register anyway — lean fully into magical fantasy framing, not horror genre

2. **Remove human victims from threat-setup shots**:
   - Show ENVIRONMENTAL DAMAGE instead — overturned market stalls, smashed pottery, burning braziers, scattered brass mugs, broken cart wheels, ember-lit streets
   - Material destruction conveys menace without tripping victim filters
   - Save protective beats for AFTER hero arrives (knockback shots are fine — NEVER-KILL still applies)

3. **NO children in peril, EVER**. Children may appear as RESCUED (after hero saved them, calm, with parent) but NEVER as in-progress victims being attacked, stalked, or cornered. Adult villagers may appear briefly in non-peril contexts (background fleeing, rescued in arms).

4. **Use "magical confrontation" language for combat**:
   - "Magical chi strike" not "violent attack"
   - "Knocked back by chi pressure" not "struck with blow"
   - "Hero intercepts the corrupted figure" not "hero attacks the villager"
   - "Corruption energy disperses" not "villager bleeds out / dies / collapses"

5. **Eye / face language**:
   - "Warm amber-red glowing eyes" instead of "blazing crimson eyes"
   - "Fierce intense expression" instead of "twisted predatory grin"
   - "Focused glare with magical energy" instead of "snarling rage"

6. **Avoid death/dying/killing/wound/bleed/scream/terror words entirely**. Replace with:
   - "Knocked back" / "Pushed back" / "Hurled back"
   - "Magical chi disperses" / "Energy dissipates"
   - "Staggers", "Recoils", "Falls back"
   - "Wide-eyed" instead of "terrified"
   - "Tense" instead of "screaming"

7. **Test cautiously**: If a prompt fails the safety check, isolate the panel that's most likely the trigger (usually the threat-setup panel) and rewrite with the playbook above before retrying.
