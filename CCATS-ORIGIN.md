## 📖 Story Premise

CCATS is a chibi anthropomorphic ginger-brown tabby cat working as a nameless factory worker at **DIVING GEAR INC.**, a massive steampunk industrial factory that manufactures deep-sea diving suit armor. His days are an endless grind — dawn arrival, punch-card ritual, the oppressive "NEVER STOP WORKING." propaganda sign looming above, assembly-line drudgery. He is uniquely weary among a routine-numb worker cat crowd.

Then one night, a cozy corner shop — **KETDONUT DONUTS & COFFEE** — changes everything. A steaming cup of coffee from young KETDONUT transforms him. His goggles glow amber, steam jets burst from his ears, his fur puffs up, his tail spins helicopter-style, his pupils become coffee beans. **CHK-CHK-PSHHHH!**

The world returns to normal. CCATS is overflowing with energy. He gulps down the entire cup and skips happily into the night. The origin of a hero begins with a single sip.

**Themes:** Chaplin's Modern Times industrial-tragedy → warm coffee-shop hope → transformation → hero origin. Chekhovian quiet build to explosive comedic transformation.

**TWO-STEP INTERACTION MECHANIC (LOCKED):**
1. Worker takes card from bib pocket
2. **INSERTS** card into slot → card slides in only HALFWAY, remaining ~50% HANGS visibly OUT of slot (like envelope in mailbox)
3. Worker **RELEASES** the card (paw no longer touches card — card hangs on its own by slot)
4. Paw **MOVES ACROSS** to RIGHT-SIDE brass LEVER HANDLE
5. **PULLS DOWN** the lever → at this moment card gets **SUCKED FULLY INSIDE** the machine (rapid vacuum motion, card disappears completely into slot)
6. CLUNK-CLANK punch stamp sound + tiny steam puff from vent
7. Slot now empty — **card stays inside machine permanently** (never retrieved)
8. Worker walks forward past machine

⚠️ Critical staging: BEFORE lever pull = card hanging halfway out + paw NOT on card. DURING lever pull = card sucked in. AFTER = slot empty.

**Card orientation (LOCKED):**
- ⭐ Card is a plain PAPER TIME-CARD — cream cardstock with printed tally-mark grid + column lines
- ⛔ NO envelope, NO letter format — just bare cardstock
- ⭐ Card enters slot **BOTTOM-EDGE FIRST** (short bottom edge slides in), with **TOP-EDGE (printed header/grid) STICKING OUT** and remaining VISIBLE above the slot lip
- ⛔ NEVER top-edge first (hides the printed info)
- ⛔ NEVER sideways / long-edge first
- Orientation while halfway-inserted: **vertical portrait**, bottom half INSIDE, top half UP and OUT
