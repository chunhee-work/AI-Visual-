## BLOCK 1 — SHARED STYLE (premium painterly 3D animation base register)

**EN:**
```
SHARED STYLE: a high-end cinematic film still — the single most dramatic hero frame of a major-studio 3D animated feature. Dramatic, MOODY, high-contrast chiaroscuro lighting: one strong warm directional key light carving the subject out of deep rich shadow, bright golden highlights falling to near-black shadow, strong rim/edge light separating the subject, clear pools of light and dark — NOT flat, NOT evenly lit, NOT bright-and-soft. Dense volumetric god-rays and thick atmospheric haze with physical density — visible floating dust motes, fine embers and drifting particles in the air. Layered depth staging with a tack-sharp foreground subject and creamy soft-focus midground and background, a strong sense of depth. Rich warm filmic color grade — deep honeyed amber and gold, crushed cool shadows, high dynamic range, cinematic contrast. Ultra-fine detail: fur groomed strand by strand with flyaways and light through the edges, realistic fabric weave and worn materials, micro surface detail, soft subsurface scattering on skin, soulful expressive eyes with crisp catchlights and real moisture. Cinematic full-frame ~35-50mm look, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh, subtle anamorphic feel, gentle lens flares, fine film grain, soft vignette, period-drama framing. All characters rendered as appealing stylized-but-realistic modern 3D animated feature-film characters (Pixar/Disney/DreamWorks register), hand-painted painterly finish — NOT photoreal real animals or real humans. Ultra-detailed, 8K, ray-traced global illumination, volumetric lighting — a frame pulled straight from a high-end animated feature.
```

## BLOCK 2 — SOFT CINEMATIC LIGHTING (the override that dials back the drama)

**EN:**
```
SOFT CINEMATIC LIGHTING AND COLOR-GRADE PASS — naturalistic, restrained, premium. Keep every character, outfit, pose, expression, prop, environment, and composition 100% identical. Change ONLY the light, color temperature, and atmosphere. The mood is "quietly cinematic" — refined, not theatrical.

TARGET STYLE: Painterly cinematic in the register of Puss in Boots: The Last Wish — premium hand-rendered 3D animation. Soft, art-directed, painterly. NOT photoreal. NOT flat illustration. NOT golden-hour-poster.

LIGHT DIRECTION
- One clear motivated key light per scene, naturally derived from the source (sky, window, lantern, screen, fire).
- Soft falloff — light gradually transitions to shadow, never harsh.
- Faces and primary subjects gently sculpted by directional light — clear shape, but not chiaroscuro-dramatic.

COLOR & GRADE
- Natural color balance, true to the scene's actual time of day. Do NOT push warm/sunset unless the source already is.
- Slight warm midtones, slight cool shadows — gentle warm/cool contrast.
- Restrained saturation — clean painterly colors, never neon or pushed.
- Sky color stays close to the source.

PRACTICAL LIGHTS
- Any visible light source (lantern, candle, lamp, window, screen, magical glow) emits a soft warm halo on nearby surfaces — present, motivated, but never dominant.

RIM / EDGE LIGHT
- A subtle soft rim light traces the back/top edges of primary subjects (hair, fur, shoulders, garment edge). Just enough to lift them off the background — barely there, never a hero highlight.

SHADOWS
- Soft, readable, slightly cool-temperature. Never crushed to black, never washed out. Maintain clean dynamic range.

ATMOSPHERE & DEPTH
- Faint atmospheric haze in the deep background — distant elements (mountains, towers, sky) gently desaturate and cool with distance, creating natural aerial perspective.
- A few drifting dust motes or gentle volumetric breath in the key-light beam — minimal, not heavy. No theatrical god-rays.
- Foreground reads clear and warm; background recedes into soft painterly distance.

SURFACE TREATMENT
- Skin, fur, hair, fabric: natural subsurface scattering — warm translucent quality at edges, ears, fingertips. Soft micro-texture, painterly hand-rendered.
- Eyes: clean directional catch-lights, gentle ambient reflection.
- No plastic CG sheen. No oversharpened photoreal detail. No flat illustration look.

OVERALL
The result should look like the same exact scene shot by a thoughtful cinematographer — slightly more sculpted, slightly more depth, slightly cleaner color — but never transformed or dramatized. Refined, elegant, premium. Same composition. Same characters. Same world. Just softly and beautifully lit.
```

## HOW TO READ THE TWO BLOCKS TOGETHER

The two blocks INTENTIONALLY contradict each other in lighting/color — that's the point.

- **Block 1 (SHARED STYLE)** establishes the premium painterly 3D animated feature BASE register: appealing stylized-but-realistic Pixar/Disney/DreamWorks finish, hand-painted, ~35-50mm cinematic look, shallow DOF, fine film grain, ultra-detailed fur/skin/fabric, soulful eyes. It sets the QUALITY/CRAFT bar with its "dramatic hero frame" framing.
- **Block 2 (SOFT CINEMATIC LIGHTING)** OVERRIDES Block 1's lighting/color/atmosphere instructions with the actual target — naturalistic, restrained, "quietly cinematic," soft falloff, gentle warm/cool, minimal atmosphere, no theatrical god-rays, soft readable shadows.

**Together:** premium painterly 3D animated feature craft + naturalistic soft lighting = the trailer's look.

**If the two blocks conflict, Block 2 always wins** (it's the "refined override" applied to Block 1's "dramatic base"). Image model interprets the contrast and lands in the middle-but-leaning-soft sweet spot.
