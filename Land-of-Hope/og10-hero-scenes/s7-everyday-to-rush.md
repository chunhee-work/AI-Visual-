## Locked rules across all S7 prompts

- **Visual style**: Auto-paste the two-block LOCKED style at start (SHARED STYLE + SOFT CINEMATIC LIGHTING PASS) per `land-of-hope-locked-visual-style.md`
- **No text**: Strict no-text rule — signage, plaques, scroll labels, jacket patches all blank or carved symbols only
- **No gore**: Destruction is atmospheric (lava-cracks in cobblestones, red-glowing windows, smoke columns) only — no bodies, child-friendly
- **Direction continuity**: All 10 heroes face the SAME burning village square; their paths visibly converge
- **Lighting through-line**: Red blood-fire glow from ahead (off-frame village) is the universal secondary key across all 10 — the unifying visual thread
- **16:9**: All shots
- **Time**: Deep night, two moons in cool purple-blue cloudy sky, floating islands ringing the valley
