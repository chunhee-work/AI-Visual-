**Paper Dragon design (locked 2026-06-15 per canonical image):**
- GIANT ORIGAMI PAPER DRAGON — massive elongated geometric dragon shape (~10-15m long)
- Constructed from CLEAN WHITE/CREAM FOLDED PAPER in TRIANGULAR ORIGAMI SCALE-PLATE SEGMENTS along the entire body (polygonal origami construction with sharp angular folded triangular plate scales running down spine + body + tail)
- ⚠️ NO KANJI / NO CALLIGRAPHY TEXT / NO INK-STROKE BRUSHWORK ON DRAGON BODY — the paper is CLEAN, just folded geometric origami construction without any writing/text markings (kanji like "自由" appear ONLY on the SCROLLS, NEVER on the dragon body itself) — locked 2026-06-15
- FLOWING RIBBON BODY — segmented serpentine Chinese dragon shape, sinuous body
- ORIGAMI FACE — sharp angular paper-folded dragon head with WARM ORANGE-AMBER GLOWING EYES + horns + folded paper whiskers
- WINGS or wingless — wingless ribbon-dragon that flies via chi-flow + serpentine swimming through air (Eastern dragon style)
- GOLD CHI AURA around dragon (chi-glow emanating around body + trailing behind it as it flies — chi is AROUND the dragon, NOT painted/written on body — locked 2026-06-15)
- Paper-fold seams visible across all triangular scale segments
- Paper-crane-flock companion: smaller origami cranes flock around the dragon as escort (also clean paper, no kanji)
