# 🎬 Land of Hope · Bounty Board — Promo Video

**Project:** Land of Hope (OG10) community promo
**Setting:** EARTH-mode (this is Earth) — see canonical [[og10-earth-mode-canonical]]
**Location:** Land of Hope town — Village Fountain Plaza + one main street (see [[village-fountain-plaza-canonical]])
**Theme / message:** From "everyone only cleans their own porch" → **working together, gotong-royong style**. Payoff reveal = the **Community Bounty Board**.
**Cast:** full OG10 lineup ([[og10-group-lineup-canonical]]) — CCATS, FOT, ZEYO, ECOX, FROAK (Fr0ak), GST, HEARTA, LYCHEE, KETDONUT, SKET.

---

## 📝 SCRIPT (as briefed)

### Opening scene — the problem, one continuous crane-down
- Beautiful morning: warm sunshine, birds chirping, gentle wind.
- **ONE continuous camera move (top → down):** start high in the SKY → tilt/craning down to reveal the MOUNTAIN (from far) → continue down into the TOWN, landing on one vibrant street with **kids playing** and a lively town scene. All in a single unbroken shot.
- Then the ugly truth on that street: **netizens/townsfolk only clean their OWN porch** — sweeping their house rubbish OUT onto the public street with brooms, pouring dirty laundry water onto the street, wetting the ground; **kids run through the dirty water.**
- **CCATS, FOT, ZEYO and ECOX** (the four are walking to get coffee) happen to pass by, see this mess, and **shake their heads.**

### Scene 2 — the plan
- **FROAK (Fr0ak) and GST** work together preparing a **written notice** at a garage-looking workspace.
- **HEARTA, LYCHEE and KETDONUT** put the notice up on a **big bulletin board** in the central plaza — ⛔ its content is NOT readable yet (kept hidden).
- **SKET** calls everyone in town together and gets ready to make an announcement — in his fierce, commanding **samurai tone** (Japanese-flavored / cat vocalizations, no subtitles).

### Scene 3 — the rally
- **SKET announces** that everyone should **work together, gotong-royong style.**
- The whole town **nods** as they listen — buy-in, unity.

### Final scene — the reveal
- Reveal that the big bulletin notice is the **COMMUNITY BOUNTY BOARD.**
